# jETHs FAQ

## What is jETHs?
jETHs is the first Liquid Staking Token (LST) Index Fund on Ethereum, providing diversified ETH exposure through a mix of wstETH, cbETH, and rETH.

## How does it work?
When you deposit WETH, you receive jETHs tokens representing your share of the index fund. The fund automatically rebalances between the three major LSTs to optimize returns and maintain security.

## What is the minimum deposit?
The minimum deposit is 0.05 ETH.

## What is the deposit cap?
The current deposit cap is **100 ETH** as we launch in a controlled manner to ensure security and stability. As the protocol matures and undergoes additional auditing, we will progressively increase the cap toward our maximum of **10,000 ETH**. Cap increases will be announced on our Twitter.

## What are the fees?
- **Performance Fee**: 10% of profits only
- **Management Fee**: None
- **Deposit/Withdrawal Fee**: None (only gas costs)

## How do I get WETH?
You can wrap ETH or swap other tokens for WETH on [Uniswap](https://app.uniswap.org/swap?chain=mainnet&outputCurrency=0xC02aaA39b223FE8D0A0e5C4F27eAD9083C756Cc2).

## What is the expected APY?
The strategy targets 3-5% APY through LST yield and rebalancing gains.
